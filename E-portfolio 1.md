

**ARTEFACT 1** 

**“SOPA: a framework for sustainability-oriented process analysis and re-design in business process management” - Klessascheck, F., Weber, I. and Pufahl, L. (2025)**

This article helped me understand that business process analysis can be used to identify opportunities for improving processes while also considering environmental sustainability. I learned that BPM involves analysing, designing and improving business processes, and that process analysis can examine factors such as cost, time, quality and environmental impact (Klessascheck, Weber and Pufahl, 2025, p. 495). The article demonstrates this learning through the SOPA framework, which combines Life Cycle Assessment and Activity-Based Costing to assess the environmental impact of individual process activities (Klessascheck, Weber and Pufahl, 2025, pp. 491–492). I selected this artefact because it shows how business process simulation can be used to evaluate different redesign options before implementing changes in a real organisation (Klessascheck, Weber and Pufahl, 2025, pp. 496–497). This makes it meaningful evidence of my understanding of process analysis and continuous process improvement.
